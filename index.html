    • General Disclaimers

  • Data coverage varies across states due to differences in agency responsibilities (e.g., ACT Roads vs ACT Police)
  • Not all police enforcement data is made public. Some states provide both police and camera data; others only provide partial or separate data sources (e.g., NSW Revenue vs NSW Police)

    • Queensland:

  • Queensland data submits their Speeding Fines in January for the whole year. For our charts, we took the data from queensland and found the average for each month
  • Not all camera detections lead to fines; each is reviewed by staff
  • Data includes various camera types (mobile, fixed, digital, combined speed/red light)

    • New South Wales

  • Pre-2019 data only includes fixed and mobile speed cameras; excludes red-light-related speed fines
  • Speed band categories changed on 1 July 2009
  • As of June 2023, fines are issued by various RMS camera types across three speed bands

    • Tasmania

  • No civilian camera operators were used in 2012
  • Detection numbers affected by outdated equipment and software issues

    • Western Australia

  • Uses multiple detection methods: on-the-spot, mobile, fixed, average speed, and red light cameras
